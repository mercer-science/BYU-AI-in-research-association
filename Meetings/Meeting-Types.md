# Meeting Types

Thursdays, 6:00 pm (time tentative), about one hour. Every meeting opens with 10 minutes of AI news.

## Training

Hands-on sessions on specific AI tools and workflows, such as coding agents, making
figures, literature search and drafting papers.

## Discussion

Research labs present how they use AI, or the group discusses a topic such as the
strengths and limits of different AI models, research ethics, data privacy, or AI and
the gospel.

## Guest Speakers

Faculty, senior PhD students and professionals who use AI in their work.
