# Email to Faculty with Research Labs

**Subject:** AI in Research Chapter: Invitation for Your Lab to Join

Dear Dr. [Last Name],

Generative AI is spreading at an unprecedented pace. ChatGPT reached an estimated 100 million monthly users just two months after launch (UBS analysis reported by Reuters, February 2023), while the internet took four years after opening to the public to reach 50 million users (U.S. Department of Commerce, *The Emerging Digital Economy*, 1998). This acceleration will fundamentally rework how research is conceived, conducted, and communicated.

We are forming an AI in Research chapter of the BYU AI Association for undergraduate and graduate student researchers in the College of Physical and Mathematical Sciences and the College of Life Sciences. Members will meet Thursday evenings at 6:00 pm (time tentative) to share how they use AI in their research, learn AI tools together and hear from guest speakers.

Dr. Grant Jensen, Dean of the College of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, has encouraged the idea as a way for students to collaborate and learn from each other. The chapter will also help students keep up with the latest developments in AI and use them to advance their research.

Given your lab's work, we would appreciate your help:

1. Please let your students know about the chapter.
2. Sometime in the coming year, would one of your students be willing to present how your lab uses AI at a meeting (about 20 minutes)?
