# BYU Requirements

The chapter will be chartered as a student academic association under the BYU
[Clubs and Associations Policy](https://policy.byu.edu/view/clubs-and-associations-policy)
and will follow the [Speakers and Events Policy](https://policy.byu.edu/view/speakers-and-events-policy).

Dr. Grant Jensen, Dean of the College of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, has expressed support for the chapter.
